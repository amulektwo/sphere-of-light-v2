# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Seekers who arrive from a scroll shared on social media or from a search about God's plan, the premortal world, or the scriptures. Most are on a phone, often late at night, reading alone. They are not shopping. They are testing whether this place is serious.

## Product Purpose

The home of The Sphere of Light, a temple library of sealed scrolls and books by AMULEK ONE. The page has two jobs: let a seeker receive the free Scroll 144 by email, and let a ready reader acquire Book II, The First Dark God. Success is a seeker who enters the library and keeps reading.

## Brand Personality

Reverent, authoritative, luminous. It invites; it never lectures. It should feel like stepping from a dark night into a lit reading room built for sacred records: quiet, old, precise, and warm.

## Anti-references

- Generic spiritual-wellness sites: soft-focus light beams, raised-arm silhouettes, pastel gradients, stock photos of people meditating.
- SaaS landing pages: icon-title-subtitle card rows, hero metrics, testimonials, rounded pill buttons.
- Anything that shows a human face, uses purple or neon, or borrows another publisher's words, symbols, or artwork.

## Design Principles

1. The record speaks first. Typography and the sealed copy carry the page; decoration serves them.
2. Every symbol is ours and means something: twelve gates, the foursquare city, the Tree of Life with white-gold fruit.
3. Truth over persuasion. No invented numbers, ratings, or testimonials; nothing claims to work that does not.
4. Reverent restraint in motion: slow, rare, and never on a reader who has asked for less.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text 18–19px with 4.5:1 contrast minimum, visible gold focus rings, touch targets of at least 44px, one H1, real labels, and every animation disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
